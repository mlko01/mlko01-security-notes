# CTFs & Labs

Welcome to my CTFs and practical labs section. Here I document writeups, challenges, and practical exercises from platforms like TryHackMe, Hack The Box, and specialized cybersecurity competitions.

My focus is on breaking down the exploitation chain step-by-step: enumeration, initial access, privilege escalation, and the lessons learned from each machine or challenge.

---

## Categories & Platforms

*   **TryHackMe (THM):** Rooms, paths, and guided labs (including foundational topics aligned with my journey toward the eJPT).
*   **Hack The Box (HTB):** Retired machines and technical writeups focusing on manual exploitation and realistic corporate environments.
*   **Custom Challenges:** Specialized CTF challenges, scripting automation, and cryptography/reverse engineering snippets.
