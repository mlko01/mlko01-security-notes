# Road to eJPT

My preparation path and structured study notes for the **eLearnSecurity Junior Penetration Tester (eJPT)** certification. 

---

## Core Modules & Study Areas

*   **Assessment Methodologies & Reconnaissance**
    *   Passive and active reconnaissance techniques.
    *   Enumeration strategies for web and network services.
*   **Host and Network Penetration Testing**
    *   Network scanning and mapping with Nmap.
    *   Exploitation fundamentals and vulnerability assessment.
    *   System pivoting and lateral movement basics.
*   **Web Application Penetration Testing**
    *   Core OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities.
    *   Practical exploitation of web vectors (SQLi, XSS, File Inclusion).
*   **Exploitation & Post-Exploitation**
    *   Working with Metasploit Framework.
    *   Maintaining access and data gathering.
