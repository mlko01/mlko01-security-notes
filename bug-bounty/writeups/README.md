# Bug Bounty Writeups

This section contains anonymized writeups, post-mortems, and lessons learned from bug bounty reports. 

In alignment with my philosophy, these are not just success stories; they include rejections, edge cases, and insights on what works and what gets filtered out by triage teams.

---

## Guidelines for Published Writeups

*   **Strict Anonymization:** All company names, internal tokens, proprietary code, and sensitive endpoints are completely redacted.
*   **Focus on Logic & Impact:** Explanations center around *why* the vulnerability existed, how it was validated safely, and how the business impact was demonstrated.
*   **Rejection Analysis:** Documenting why certain reports failed or got marked as "informative/Not Applicable" to improve future hunting strategies.
