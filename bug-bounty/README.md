# Bug Bounty

Welcome to my bug bounty notes. Here I document my personal approach to hunting on platforms like HackerOne, Bugcrowd, YesWeHack, and Intigriti, with a strict focus on **real-world impact** rather than automated scanner noise or theoretical findings.

---

## Philosophy

*   **Proof over Theory:** Every finding or note here is backed by an understanding of how it affects business logic or system integrity.
*   **Quality over Quantity:** I prioritize deep reconnaissance and custom-tailored payload crafting over mass-scanning.
*   **Respectful Disclosure:** All insights shared here strictly adhere to the disclosure policies of the respective platforms. No unmasked sensitive data or active zero-days are exposed.
